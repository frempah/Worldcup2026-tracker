About This App

World Cup 2026 Tracker is a free Progressive Web App built to help football fans track every match, follow group standings, visualize the knockout bracket, and make their own predictions throughout the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

The 2026 World Cup runs from June 11 – July 19, 2026 across the United States, Canada, and Mexico — the biggest World Cup in history with 48 teams and 104 matches.

Features

📅
Live Matches
Real-time scores and fixtures for every game
🗂
Group Standings
All 12 groups with live standings tables
🏆
Bracket Tracker
Full knockout bracket from R32 to the Final
🎯
Predictions
Pick your winners and track your accuracy

Data Source

Match data is powered by football-data.org — a trusted football data API used by developers worldwide.

Privacy

Your predictions are stored only on your device. We do not collect personal data. Read our full Privacy Policy.
