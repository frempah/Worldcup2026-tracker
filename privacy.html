Last updated: May 2026

We do not collect any personally identifiable information unless you choose to make a payment through Paystack. In that case, your email address is processed securely by Paystack in accordance with their privacy policy.

We store your match predictions and premium status locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.
